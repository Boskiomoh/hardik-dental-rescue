Hardik Dental: from broken booking to tested, secure and faster

A dental clinic site and admin panel generated with Lovable (React, TypeScript, Supabase), taken from an open-source MIT-licensed repo. Online booking failed for every visitor, patient billing records were open to anyone, and live secrets were committed to the repo. I logged 21 bugs, fixed 20 and added two features. Audit, fixes and features by Daniel Omoyibo, November 2026.

How it was tested

  • 49 Playwright end-to-end tests, including direct calls to the database API as each role and as an anonymous visitor, plus a full Stripe test-mode payment.
  • axe-core accessibility scans on 11 routes at 1280 px and 375 px.
  • Lighthouse 12.8.2 (mobile, median of 3 runs) on both live sites.
  • The untouched app runs against its own copy of the original database, rebuilt from the repo's migrations.
